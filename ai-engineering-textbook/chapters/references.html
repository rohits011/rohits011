Reference Library & Research Policy

The established works each part of this book draws on, the official documentation it defers to, and the rules used to keep facts, examples and opinions apart.

Research and writing policy

What the book is built from

Each part was planned against four kinds of source, in this order of authority:

  1. Official specifications and documentation (RFCs, language specs, project docs such as Kafka, PostgreSQL, Kubernetes and Spring). Behaviour and defaults are taken from here.
  2. Peer-reviewed and widely cited papers (Dynamo, Raft, Spanner, “Attention Is All You Need”, LoRA, HNSW and similar). Algorithms and their guarantees are taken from here.
  3. Established textbooks (listed below). They shaped the structure and teaching order of each part. No text, figures or exercises are reproduced from them.
  4. First-party engineering publications: company engineering blogs, conference talks and public postmortems. Case studies are taken from here and always linked to the original.

How claims are labelled

LabelMeaningHow to treat it
VerifiedSupported by the linked or cited source.Safe to rely on; still check the source for nuance.
IllustrativeNumbers, companies or scenarios invented to teach a point, e.g. capacity estimates.Never quote as fact. Re-derive with your own inputs.
OpinionEngineering judgement or a recommendation.Weigh it against your context. Reasonable experts disagree.
Version-sensitiveProduct versions, defaults, limits, prices and model names.True at the date shown. Check current docs before relying on it.

Fast-moving areas

LLM model names, context sizes, prices, agent frameworks and the MCP specification change month to month. Chapters in Part 8 were checked in November 2026. Treat every number there as a snapshot, and prefer the provider's documentation.

Copyright

All explanations, diagrams, code and exercises are original. Reference books are cited as further reading. Where a case study summarises a company's published engineering post, the summary is in our own words and the post is linked so you can read the original.
